Music Trends

How has musical success changed over 60 years? I analyzed historical music data and Spotify metrics to explore how artist popularity differs across record sales, Billboard charts, Grammy Awards, and streaming platforms.

Role

Data Analyst | Independent Project

Tools

Python, Pandas, Seaborn, Matplotlib, Spotify API, Jupyter Notebook

Data

3 historical datasets covering music sales (1960–2019), Billboard chart performance, and Grammy Awards (1999–2019), supplemented with Spotify API data.

Timeline

Spring 2024

The question

Music success can be measured in many ways, from record sales and chart rankings to industry awards and streaming popularity. I wanted to explore whether these different metrics reflect the same trends and how artist and genre popularity has shifted over time.

What I did

  1. Data Collection & API Integration: Analyzed three historical music datasets and integrated Spotify API data to compare past achievements with more recent artist popularity metrics.
  2. Data Cleaning & Processing: Used Python and Pandas to organize datasets, extract primary genres, group artist records, and aggregate chart performance and award counts.
