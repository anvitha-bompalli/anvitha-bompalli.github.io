Blossoming

How can minimal design communicate a story? I created a movie poster using only two images and 75% negative space, exploring how visual composition, typography, and simplicity can create an impactful design.

Role

Graphic designer

Tools

Photoshop

Constraints

2 images, 75% negative space

Timeline

Fall 2025

The poster

The challenge

Design a visually compelling movie poster using only two images while leaving 75% of the composition empty. The goal was to communicate a narrative and establish a distinct visual mood through minimal design elements.

Design choices

  • Visual Simplicity: Used two carefully selected images to establish a focal point without overwhelming the composition.
  • Negative Space: Emphasized empty space to draw attention to the imagery and create a clean, balanced layout.
  • Visual Hierarchy: Used typography, placement, and contrast to guide the viewer's attention and reinforce the poster's mood.
