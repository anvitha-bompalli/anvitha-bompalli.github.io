Resolution

How can social connections help people stay motivated toward their personal goals? Resolution is a wellness app concept designed to help users build routines, stay accountable, and connect with others working toward similar goals.

Role

UX researcher & designer

Tools

Figma

Methods

User Research, Persona Development, User Flows, Wireframing, Prototyping, Usability Testing

Timeline

Spring 2024

The problem

Staying consistent with personal goals can be challenging, especially without motivation or support. We wanted to explore how a wellness app could encourage accountability by connecting users with others who share similar goals and interests.

What I did

  1. User Research: Analyzed user behaviors and needs to identify motivations, challenges, and expectations surrounding personal goals and wellness.
  2. Persona Development: Synthesized research findings into user personas representing key behaviors, needs, and frustrations.
  3. User Flows & Wireframing: Designed user flows and wireframes for the matching and messaging experiences to visualize how users would connect and communicate.
  4. Prototyping & Iteration: Developed interface designs in Figma and refined navigation and iconography based on usability testing feedback.

What I learned from users

  • Motivation and accountability: Building consistent routines can be challenging, highlighting the potential value of support from others with similar goals.
  • Intuitive navigation: Clear navigation and recognizable icons are important for helping users move between features.
  • Simple communication: The matching and messaging experiences needed to be straightforward to help users connect without unnecessary complexity.

Design decisions

  • Goal-based matching: Designed a matching experience to connect users with others pursuing similar wellness goals.
  • Streamlined messaging: Designed a matching experience to connect users with others pursuing similar wellness goals.
  • Improved usability: Refined navigation and iconography based on usability testing feedback to make the interface more intuitive.
