Interactive Gender Pay Gap Analysis

What factors contribute to differences in employee compensation? I developed an interactive Power BI dashboard to explore gender-based salary differences across performance ratings, education levels, departments, and seniority.

The question

How does compensation differ between men and women, and how do factors such as job performance, education, department, and seniority relate to these differences? I wanted to explore these patterns and create an interactive way to examine pay equity across different employee groups.

What I did

  1. Data Analysis: Analyzed employee compensation data to compare salary distributions across gender, performance, education, department, and seniority.
  2. Comparative Analysis: Examined differences in compensation within employee groups to better understand how various factors relate to the overall gender pay gap.
  3. Interactive Dashboard Design: Built a Power BI dashboard with interactive filters, allowing users to explore compensation patterns across different employee characteristics.
  4. Data Visualization: Designed visualizations to communicate salary differences, highlight patterns, and make complex compensation data easier to interpret.
