Historical handyman

Where are the opportunities in New Orleans' home repair market? We analyzed customer reviews, existing competitors, and seasonal weather patterns to identify underserved needs and develop a data-informed business strategy for a specialized home repair service.

Role

Data Analyst

Tools

SQL

Data

1,000+ business reviews, competitor information, seasonal weather patters

Timeline

Fall 2023

The question

New Orleans' historic buildings and frequent severe weather create unique home maintenance challenges. We wanted to understand whether existing repair services adequately addressed these needs and identify opportunities for a business specializing in historic property restoration and emergency repairs.

What I did

  1. SQL Data Analysis: Queried a dataset of 1,000+ business reviews to investigate customer experiences and identify potential service gaps in the local repair market.
  2. Competitive Analysis: Examined existing handyman services and their offerings, including painting, drywall repair, wall patching, and general maintenance.
  3. Seasonal Demand Analysis: Considered how New Orleans' subtropical climate and June–November hurricane season could influence demand for emergency repairs and restoration services.
  4. Market Opportunity Identification: Combined customer review analysis, competitor research, and environmental factors to identify opportunities for specialized home repair services.
  5. Business Recommendations: Developed a proposed service model and launch strategy addressing both year-round historic building maintenance and seasonal emergency repairs.

Key findings

  • Specialized restoration presented a potential market opportunity. Existing competitors offered general maintenance services, suggesting an opportunity to differentiate through repairs tailored to historic properties.
  • Seasonal weather created distinct service needs. Hurricane season introduced a potential need for emergency water damage and drywall repair, while historic building maintenance offered a possible year-round service focus.
  • Service availability could be a competitive differentiator. A model combining regular business hours with 24/7 emergency availability during hurricane season could address varying customer needs.

Recommendations

  • Offer specialized services: Focus on historic property restoration and employ experienced technicians or collaborate with specialized contractors.
  • Adapt services to seasonal demand: Provide routine restoration and maintenance throughout the year, with expanded emergency repair availability during hurricane season.
  • Launch strategically: Open in May to establish local awareness and prepare operations ahead of the June hurricane season.
