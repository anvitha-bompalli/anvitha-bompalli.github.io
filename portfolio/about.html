About me!

Hi there! I'm an undergraduate student at CU Boulder, majoring in Information Science with a minor in Creative Technology and Design.

My interests lie at the intersection of design, accessibility, and data. I'm passionate about understanding how people interact with technology and using research and data-driven insights to create intuitive, inclusive, and meaningful experiences.

Through my projects, I've explored everything from user research and interface design to data analysis and visualization. I enjoy combining analytical thinking with creativity to solve problems, challenge design assumptions, and make technology more accessible to everyone.

I'm always looking for opportunities to learn, explore new perspectives, and create things that make people's everyday experiences a little better.
