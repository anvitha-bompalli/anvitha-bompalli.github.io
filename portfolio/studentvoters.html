How Do College Students Gather Information About Elections?

Where do college students get election information, and what challenges do they face when preparing to vote? We interviewed CU Boulder students to understand their information-seeking habits, voting experiences, and awareness of university resources.

Role

Researcher

Methods

Semi-Structured Interviews, Thematic Coding, Qualitative Analysis

Timeline

Fall 2023

The question

How do college students find and evaluate election information, and how can universities better support them in navigating the voting process?

What I did

  1. Interview Design: Collaborated on an interview guide and refined questions based on initial findings.
  2. User Interviews: Interviewed three CU Boulder freshmen about their voting experiences, information sources, and perceptions of bias.
  3. Thematic Analysis: Coded interview transcripts to identify recurring themes and information-seeking patterns.
  4. Research Synthesis: Combined findings with my team to develop recommendations for improving student access to voting information.

Key findings

  • Information Sources: Students with voting experience researched candidates using multiple sources rather than relying on a single outlet.
  • Voting Experience: First-time voters were less familiar with where to find reliable election information.
  • Resource Awareness: Students reported limited awareness of university voting resources outside election periods.

Recommendations for universities

  • Make voter registration, absentee ballot guidance, and election information easier to access.
  • Provide consistent, nonpartisan election resources throughout the semester rather than only near Election Day.
  • Create inclusive spaces that encourage students with different political perspectives to participate.
